# Audio credits

All sound effects and music in `public/audio/` were generated on 2026-11-15 with Stability AI
Stable Audio 3.0 (`small-sfx` for effects, `small-music` for music), open weights from Hugging Face,
under the Stability AI Community License (commercial use free below USD 1M annual revenue;
registration at stability.ai/community-license required). Outputs are owned by the game's publisher.
Per-file model, seed and date: `tools/audio/PROVENANCE.csv`. Generator: `tools/audio/kittangle_sounds.ipynb`.
Post-processing: `tools/audio/process.sh` (trim, loudness, MP3; music loops crossfaded by `loop.py`).
